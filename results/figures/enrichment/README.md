# Enrichment Figures

Curated figures from the niche/LCP gene-set enrichment analysis of GSE210616.

## Analysis

Gene-set enrichment was performed to characterize biological programs associated with the identified MLCP and SLCP spatial contexts.

The final enrichment analysis contained:

- **1,228 enrichment records**
- **68 unique enrichment terms**

The analysis included recurrent biological programs associated with processes such as:

- mTORC1 signaling
- MYC-related programs
- Oxidative phosphorylation
- Adipogenesis
- DNA repair
- E2F targets
- G2M checkpoint
- Cell-cycle regulation
- Proteasomal degradation
- Translation
- Respiration
- Secreted programs

## Included figures

This folder contains two curated summary heatmaps:

- STEP56_SLCP_X_LCP_LOG2_ENRICHMENT_HEATMAP.png

These visualize the enrichment structure for the MLCP and SLCP analyses.

The figures are curated summary visualizations. Exact enrichment records and downstream summary tables are retained in the project results and final-table outputs.
